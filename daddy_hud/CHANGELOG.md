# daddy_hud · HUD studio

Download the latest version from the [CFX Portal](https://portal.cfx.re/assets/granted-assets).

## 0.0.1

- A HUD with a studio: players pick the look of every part, move and scale it on a grid, save presets and share them with a code
- Status looks, speedometers for cars, bikes, boats, aircraft and trains, compass and minimap options
- Voice (pma-voice, mumble-voip, SaltyChat), needs, notifications, progress bar and a vehicle menu
- Servers decide what players may change; looks kept on the server per player
- Qbox, QBCore, ESX or standalone
