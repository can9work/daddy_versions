# daddy_venue · Restaurant & business management

Download the latest version from the [CFX Portal](https://portal.cfx.re/assets/granted-assets).

## 0.0.1

- Businesses with owners, staff, roles and permissions, opening hours and settings
- Menu, pricing, stock, kitchen stations, orders and a self-service kiosk
- Finance, payroll, schedules, hiring, staff performance and a dashboard
- Delivery, promotions, loyalty and reviews
- Property building with placeable objects and shells
- Bills through daddy_bills, esx_billing or your own script
- Every feature can be turned off in Config.Features
