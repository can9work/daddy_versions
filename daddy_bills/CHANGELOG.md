# daddy_bills · Bills, fines & receipts

Download the latest version from the [CFX Portal](https://portal.cfx.re/assets/granted-assets).

## 0.0.1

- Bills on a tablet: personal, job, state and business bills, items with tax, due dates, late fees, installments, recurring bills, autopay
- Objections: the customer objects, the sender or the boss of the job decides
- Show a bill to a player nearby; a receipt item for every paid bill
- Saved templates, personal and per job
- Boss screen: charts, staff, customers, bills, objections, grade rules and ready items of the job
- Admin screen: the whole city, write off, waive late fees, extend due dates, state bills
- Discord logs for every bill event and a weekly report per job
- Takes the place of esx_billing and okokBilling and copies their unpaid bills once
- ESX, QBCore, Qbox and standalone; languages en, tr, de, fr, es, pt, pl
